# Creative-Pro · Creative Production Lab

**See a direction. Bring the method into your own work.**

A creative recipe shelf for visual work, interface design, and writing. Previews, complete prompts, steps, and constraints live together, giving your next project a specific place to start.

**[Visit the website ↗](https://pprompt.online/) · [Explore recipes](https://pprompt.online/explore/) · [中文](README.md)**

## Find a starting point for the task in front of you

A saved poster, interface, or paragraph gives you inspiration. A useful recipe helps you continue: see the preview, understand the inputs and boundaries, and take the method into your own work.

| Your task | What you can take away |
| --- | --- |
| Give a photograph a new expression | Directions for illustration, collage, posters, and color treatments |
| Design a more deliberate interface | Prompts for complete pages, component specifications, forms, typography, and microinteractions |
| Write for a specific purpose | Methods for articles, spoken scripts, fiction, short dramas, revision, and review |

## 03 · Understand the method before using it

A detail page combines previews, the prompt, exclusions, and usage guidance. Skills with complete execution instructions also expose workflow, quality gates, and reuse notes. The primary action explains whether it copies a prompt or returns an image Skill to the creation desk.

## 04 · Get a concrete first move

The idea diagnostic organizes three creative intents into useful constraints and editable starter directions. Choose one, then carry it back to the homepage composer. Connected ticket stubs make the choice, content, and next action easy to distinguish.

## What you can use today

- **UI and writing recipes:** read the complete prompt and copy it into a suitable AI tool.
- **Visual recipes:** explore previews and instructions, then continue with your own materials.
- **In-site image generation:** the frontend entry exists, but generation submission and Full Skill execution are currently disabled on the production backend. This showcase does not indicate an enabled generation service.
